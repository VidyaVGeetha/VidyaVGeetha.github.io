Professional Profile

An evolving career in data, insight and technology

Vidya's career began with the Government of Kerala's rural employment programme (MGNREGS), India's largest public employment scheme. For nine years she maintained, reconciled and reported on worker, attendance and wage records, and prepared monthly progress reports and performance indicators for block officials.

Since moving to Scotland she has volunteered as a QA and data analyst with Scottish Tech Army, and now works as a Sustainability Analyst Intern at Klimado, building Python validation workflows and automating ESG data collection. She is looking for data, reporting and planning analyst roles in Scotland.
